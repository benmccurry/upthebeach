The Cottage

Ready to kick back, relax, and live the beach life? Welcome to "Up the Beach"—your new favorite getaway on the laid-back west end of Panama City Beach.

This cozy cottage brings a perfect mix of old Florida charm and modern comforts, all while being pet-friendly (for up to 2 dogs under 60 pounds for an additional fee). Whether you're lounging in the living room or strolling down to the beach, this 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom gem is your home away from home.

Just a quick 0.2 miles from the beach, you'll love how easy it is to get your toes in the sand. Inside, you'll find a bright, spacious living room with plush seating and a 65" HD TV, a fully stocked kitchen with both a Keurig and drip coffee maker, and dining for six. The fenced side patio provides a private outdoor retreat.

The downstairs features a king bedroom with a 50" SMART TV and a full bathroom with a standalone shower. Upstairs includes a cozy office nook with a desk, reading chair, TV, and monitor, along with two additional bedrooms. The king master suite features a private balcony, while the queen bedroom offers another comfortable retreat.

Family-friendly amenities include a pack-and-play and high chair. Outside, guests can enjoy the fenced yard and propane grill. Additional beach gear such as bikes and paddleboards is available through a local partner with a stay-based credit.

Laguna Beach

Up the Beach and Beyond…

Nestled in the quiet charm of Laguna Beach, Up the Beach offers the perfect balance of laid-back coastal living and easy access to some of Panama City Beach's most loved destinations.

Whether you're here to unwind by the water, explore charming coastal towns, or enjoy local dining, everything you need is just minutes away.

Nearby

Local Highlights

Before or after a beach day, guests can enjoy Thomas' Donuts, Carousel Supermarket, Pier Park, Shipwreck Island Waterpark, Gulf World, The Hub 30A, Destin Commons, Sandestin Outlet Mall, and numerous restaurants and grocery stores nearby.

The Setting

About the Area

The West End of Panama City Beach offers a quieter atmosphere with beautiful white sand beaches and emerald Gulf waters. Nearby attractions include Camp Helen State Park and Conservation Park, along with opportunities for paddleboarding, kayaking, snorkeling, dining, shopping, and exploring local coastal communities.
